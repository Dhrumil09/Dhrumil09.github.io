Common questions

I signed up but it says "Waiting for approval".
Ledgerly is invite-only. The owner approves new accounts; the screen updates by itself once you're approved.

How do I email an invoice from my phone?
Open the invoice and tap Email. Your phone's mail app opens with the PDF attached; tap Send.

Can I use Ledgerly on my computer too?
Yes. Sign in with the same email and password on Mac or Windows and your data is there.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account → Delete my account. Export your reports first, as tax records should be kept for five years.
