LEDGERLY

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Ledgerly is an invoicing app for Australian small businesses, available for iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows. This policy explains what information Ledgerly stores, why, and the choices you have.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and a securely stored password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication — we never see your password).
  • Your business details: business name, ABN, address, phone, email, bank details for payment instructions, logo, invoice design and settings — whatever you choose to enter.
  • Your customers, products, invoices, quotes and recurring schedules: the records you create in the app, including your customers' names and contact details.
  • An email log: when you email an invoice or quote, a record of when it was sent and to which address.

Ledgerly does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to show your data on your devices, keep them in sync, create PDFs, and work out GST and reports. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (part of Google Firebase) in the australia-southeast2 (Melbourne) region, and is encrypted in transit and at rest. Google acts as our service provider under its own privacy and security terms.

Emails you send

On the phone, invoices are emailed through your own mail app, so the email comes from your address. On a computer, you may connect your own email account; its app password is stored only on that computer, encrypted by the operating system, and is never uploaded.

Photos

If you choose a logo from your photo library, only that one image is resized and saved to your business details. Ledgerly does not access your other photos.

Who can see your data

Only you, when signed in to your account. Access to Ledgerly is by approval; the app owner can approve, pause or restore an account's access but does not use your business records for any other purpose.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your business, invoices, quotes, customers, products, schedules and sign-in. Australian tax law generally requires businesses to keep records for five years, so export your reports before deleting.

Children

Ledgerly is a business tool and is not intended for children under 16.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles you can ask to access or correct your personal information, or make a complaint. Most information can be viewed and edited directly in the app. For anything else, contact us below. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
