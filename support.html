Support

My steps don't turn into light

Check Settings → Health → Data Access → Lumitree and turn on Steps. Steps sync when you open the app; up to 6 days back are counted.

A friend took my light

Gather orbs within the grace hour after they ripen, or put a shield on your next orb. You can take light back within an hour.

My tree wasn't approved

You get your credit back. Retake the photos in daylight with the tree filling the close-up, from where it's planted.

Deleting your account

You → Settings → Delete account.
